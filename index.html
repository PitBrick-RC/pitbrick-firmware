Current version / Huidige versie / Aktuelle Version: 2.10 (23 Nov 2026)
  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (not Safari, not a phone).
  2. Switch the PitBrick on and connect it with a USB-C data cable.
  3. Click the button below, choose the PitBrick port and click Install.
  4. Wait until it says it is done (about 1 minute). Your settings stay saved.
This browser cannot do this. Please use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened via https.
